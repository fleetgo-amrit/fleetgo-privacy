FleetGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

FleetGo helps drivers, vehicle owners and fleet managers manage trips, fuel, expenses, maintenance, vehicle documents and related records. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information the app may handle and how that information may be used.

1. Information the App Handles

Depending on the features you use and the information you provide, FleetGo may handle:

  • Account information: Email address and account details used to sign in. If Google Sign-In is used, relevant Google account information may also be processed.
  • Profile information: Details such as name, phone number, driving licence number and vehicle or truck number, when entered.
  • Operational records: Trips, fuel entries, expenses, income, maintenance, attendance, salary, leave, chat messages and emergency contacts, where those features are used.
  • Photos and documents: Images or files uploaded for vehicle documents, driver documents, receipts, attendance, profile photos or vehicle photos, where supported.
  • Location information: The Accident Alert feature uses the device's GPS location. Live Tracking may also use location information. The collection, transmission and retention of location information depend on the implementation of these features.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide app features, sign users in and manage accounts, display and maintain vehicle and fleet records, support trip, fuel, expense and maintenance workflows, provide location-related features where enabled, and keep cloud-backed records available across supported devices when users sign in to the same account.

3. Data Storage and Service Providers

The current project configuration and code indicate the use of Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Firebase-related cloud storage configuration. Firestore is also referenced in the app's code. The exact data stored by each service, the location of stored data and any additional services involved must be confirmed against the final released app configuration.

For information about Google's privacy practices, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Location Information

FleetGo's Accident Alert feature requests the device's current GPS location. Location-related features may require location permission from the device. This policy does not claim that location information is never transmitted to or stored on a server. The final handling of location information must be confirmed by testing the released app and reviewing its code and connected services.

Users can review and change location permissions in their device settings. Disabling permission may prevent location-related features from working.

5. Data Sharing

FleetGo uses or is configured to use third-party services to provide app functionality. Information processed by those services depends on the features used and the app's configuration. We do not make a blanket claim that information is never shared with other third parties until all integrated services and data flows have been reviewed. For privacy-related questions, contact us using the email address below.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

FleetGo records may be stored in cloud services or on the device, depending on the feature and implementation. An in-app account-deletion feature is planned for implementation before the public release. Until that feature and the associated data-deletion process have been implemented and tested, this policy does not claim that users can currently delete their entire account and all associated data through the app.

Before public release, this section must be updated to explain how users can request account deletion, how deletion requests are processed, and whether any data must be retained for legitimate reasons.

7. Security

FleetGo relies on the security features and configuration of the app and its connected services. Appropriate access controls should be verified before release, particularly for fleet records, salary information, identity documents, photos and location information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. App Lock and Device Permissions

Some features may depend on device-level security settings or permissions. The exact behavior of any app-lock or biometric feature must be confirmed against the released version.

9. Children's Privacy

FleetGo is intended for adult drivers, vehicle owners and fleet-management users. It is not intended for children under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FleetGo's features, connected services or data practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
