# Delete your FleetGo account

You can delete your FleetGo account and data at any time.

## In the app
1. Open FleetGo and sign in.
2. Go to Settings, then Delete my account.
3. Enter your password (or confirm with Google), type DELETE and confirm.

## What is deleted
Your login account and your fleet data: trips, fuel, expenses, income, maintenance, attendance, salary, leave, chat messages, profile, truck information and photos. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

## If you cannot use the app
Email 22asdhillon@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my FleetGo account". We will delete your account and data and confirm by email.

## Notes
- If you are a member of another fleet, or your fleet has other members, in-app deletion is not available yet. Email us and we will help.
- Copies held by our service provider (Google Firebase) may take some time to be fully removed.
