
# FleetGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

FleetGo helps drivers and fleet owners record trips, fuel, expenses, maintenance, documents and related records.

Contact: 22asdhillon@gmail.com

## 1. Information We Collect

FleetGo may process information you provide, such as your email address, your name, phone number, license details, trip records, attendance, and expense entries, to support fleet management features.

## 2. Location Data

FleetGo uses location permissions for features such as live tracking and accident alerts. Location access may depend on the feature being used and the device permissions granted.

## 3. Photos and Documents

Photos and documents you submit, including attendance selfies and vehicle documents, may be processed for attendance, verification, and fleet record-keeping.

## 4. Data Storage

The app uses Firebase services for certain app functions. The information stored depends on the features you use and the data you submit.

## 5. Data Sharing

Personal information may be accessible to authorized fleet owners or managers where required for fleet operations. Information may also be processed by service providers used by the app.
